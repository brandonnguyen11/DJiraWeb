Frequently asked questions

Why real-time vs. a regular AI notetaker?
Real-time AI helps you in the moment — giving you the right answer when someone asks a tough question, not just a summary afterward. It's the difference between having a brilliant advisor in your ear vs. reading a transcript later.
Who is Cluely for?
Cluely is designed for sales professionals, executives, consultants, recruiters, and anyone who wants to perform better in meetings and calls. Whether you're negotiating deals or conducting interviews, Cluely helps you stay sharp.
Is Cluely free?
Cluely offers a free tier with limited usage. Paid plans unlock unlimited AI responses, full transcription, follow-up email generation, and more. Visit our pricing page for details.
How is it undetectable in meetings?
Cluely uses OS-level screen capture techniques that are invisible to screen-sharing software. The overlay is rendered in a way that doesn't appear in shared screens on Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, or other platforms.
What languages and apps are supported?
Cluely supports 12+ languages including English, Spanish, Mandarin, French, German, Japanese, and more. It works with all major video conferencing apps: Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, Slack, and RingCentral.
Can I talk to customer support?
Yes! We have a dedicated support team available via our Help Center and email. Pro and Enterprise customers get priority support with faster response times.
